About Bloomlinx

Not just another introduction. A thoughtful connection.

The right creator partnership is about more than follower counts. It starts with understanding a brand, its products, its audience and the story it wants to tell.

Bloomlinx Agency is an early-stage beauty creator marketing agency being built in Nigeria with an international outlook. We focus on identifying relevant creators and developing partnership ideas that bring brands and creative talent together.

Our goal is to make creator collaborations more organized, considered and accessible, while helping creators discover legitimate opportunities to work with beauty brands.
